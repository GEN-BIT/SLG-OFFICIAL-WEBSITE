Featured Campus Locations

🏗️

Software Development Lab

State-of-the-art computer lab with 30+ workstations, latest development tools, and high-speed internet. A space where students code real-world applications.

⚡

Electrical Technology Workshop

Fully equipped workshop with power distribution systems, control panels, and testing equipment for hands-on electrical training and installations.

🔌

Electronics Lab

Modern laboratory with oscilloscopes, multimeters, and component testing stations where students design and test electronic circuits.

🏢

Construction Workshop

Practical building construction facility featuring model structures, welding stations, and materials testing equipment for construction engineering students.

📚

Library & Learning Center

Modern library with over 5,000 books, computers, study areas, and high-speed WiFi for research and academic support.

🏠

Student Dormitories

Comfortable accommodation for male and female students with modern amenities, study rooms, and recreational facilities.

🍽️

Dining Hall & Cafeteria

Spacious dining facility serving nutritious meals three times daily, with separate study and relaxation areas for students.

⚽

Sports & Recreation Center

Outdoor sports fields, volleyball court, basketball court, and recreational areas for student clubs and physical activities.
