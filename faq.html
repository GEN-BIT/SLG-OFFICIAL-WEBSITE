Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about admissions, programs, and student life at Saint Laurent Gaseke TSS.

To be admitted to Saint Laurent Gaseke TSS, applicants must have:

  • Completed primary or secondary education
  • Passed the relevant national exams (PLE/O-Level)
  • Be at least 16 years old
  • Provide original academic transcripts and certificates

The school opens admissions twice yearly:

  • First Intake: January — April
  • Second Intake: July — September

We recommend applying as early as possible as spaces are limited.

You can apply online through our website, or visit the school office with your original documents. The application fee is RWF 15,000. Submit your application to our admissions office along with:

  • Completed application form
  • Academic certificates and transcripts
  • Birth certificate or national ID
  • Two passport-size photos
  • Application fee receipt

We offer four nationally accredited TVET Level 4 programs, each lasting three years:

  • Software Development (SOD) — Web and mobile app development
  • Electrical Technology (ETE) — Power systems and installations
  • Electronics (ELE) — Electronic devices and circuits
  • Building Construction (BDC) — Structural and civil works

All our programs feature 80–90% practical, hands-on training. Students spend significant time in workshops and labs, gaining real-world skills that employers value. This practical focus ensures graduates are job-ready upon completion.

Program changes are possible only within the first month of the academic year, subject to approval by the school administration. A request letter must be submitted to the Principal's office with justification. Limited spots may be available depending on capacity.

Yes, the school provides student accommodation. Dormitories are available for both male and female students on a first-come, first-served basis. Accommodation fees are additional to tuition fees. Contact our admissions office for current rates and availability.

We encourage student involvement in various activities including:

  • Sports clubs (football, volleyball, basketball, athletics)
  • Cultural and music groups
  • Technology and innovation clubs
  • Community service initiatives
  • Student government associations

The school maintains a code of conduct to ensure a safe and respectful learning environment. Discipline is progressive and fair. Minor infractions result in warnings, while serious misconduct may lead to suspension or expulsion. All students receive a handbook outlining expectations and consequences.

Tuition fees vary by program and are payable annually or in installments. Contact our admissions office for current fee schedules. Scholarships and financial aid are available for qualified students based on academic merit and financial need.

Yes! We offer merit-based and need-based scholarships. Interested students should:

  • Submit a scholarship application form
  • Provide academic transcripts
  • Include a personal statement explaining financial need
  • Submit supporting documentation

Scholarship decisions are made during the admissions review process.

Yes, flexible payment plans are available. Students can arrange payment schedules with our finance office. All tuition must be paid in full before the end of each academic year to remain enrolled. Contact the finance office to discuss payment options.
