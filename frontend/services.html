Our Technical Programs

Four nationally accredited TVET Level 4 programs — each three years with 80–90% practical training.

💻
SOD — Dept. 01

Software Development

Programming, web development and custom software — building Rwanda's digital economy.

Web Development HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript and responsive website design.
Database Management MySQL, data modelling and backend administration.
Programming Fundamentals Python, PHP and software engineering principles.
Computer Networking IP configuration, LAN setup and network administration.
Real Client Projects School portals, inventory systems and business apps.
IT Support & Hardware Troubleshooting, OS installation and technical support.

Website Design School Management Systems Database Solutions IT Consulting PC Repair Network Setup

ELE — Dept. 02

Electrical Technology

Industrial and domestic electrical systems, solar energy and power infrastructure across Rwanda.

Domestic Installation House wiring, circuit breakers, lighting and safety compliance.
Industrial Wiring Three-phase systems, motor controls and factory infrastructure.
Solar & Renewable Energy Solar PV installation, charge controllers and inverters.
Electrical Safety Standards International safety codes, testing and hazard prevention.
PLC & Automation Basics Introduction to programmable logic controllers.
Blueprint Reading Electrical drawing interpretation and schematic documentation.

ETE — Dept. 03

Electronics

Repair, maintenance and design of electronic systems — a high-demand skill across Rwanda.

Circuit Design & Analysis Analogue and digital circuits, PCB design, component ID.
Consumer Electronics Repair TV, radio, audio equipment and mobile phone servicing.
Industrial Control Systems Sensors, actuators and electronic control panels.
Microcontrollers & Arduino Embedded systems and automation project builds.
Testing Equipment Oscilloscopes, multimeters and signal generators.
Renewable Energy Electronics Solar charge controllers, inverters and off-grid systems.

BDC — Dept. 04

Building Construction

Training skilled masons, site supervisors and construction consultants for Rwanda's growing sector.

Masonry & Bricklaying Foundation laying, wall construction, plastering and tiling.
Structural Design Basics Architectural drawings, calculations and load analysis.
Site Management Construction planning, materials management and on-site safety.
Concrete Technology Mix design, formwork, reinforced concrete and quality testing.
Roofing Systems Timber roofing, iron sheet installation and waterproofing.
Plumbing Basics Drainage systems, water supply and sanitation infrastructure.
